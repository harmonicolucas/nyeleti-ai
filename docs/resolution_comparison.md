## Size of the problem

A cell-day is MODIS-informed when MODIS has a detection in the cell or in one of its eight neighbours within one day, so the neighbourhood grows with the cell: 3 km across at 1 km, 15 km at 5 km, 30 km at 10 km. VIIRS detections outside informed cell-days are only covered by the background rate.
